Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At MD Enterprise , we strive to provide the best on-site services and Repair packages. Please read our refund and cancellation policy carefully before making any online payment.

1. Cancellation by Customer

  • Service Bookings: If you wish to cancel a scheduled service visit, you must inform us at least 24 hours before the scheduled time. You can cancel by calling us at +91 99185-82184.
  • Repair Packages: You may cancel your Repair package within 3 days of purchase if no service visits have been utilized. After 3 days or after utilizing a visit, the package cannot be cancelled.

2. Refunds for Online Payments

  • If a cancellation request is approved, the refund will be initiated within 24-48 hours.
  • The refunded amount will be credited back to the original payment method (UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or Net Banking) used during the transaction.
  • It may take 5-7 working days for the amount to reflect in your bank account, depending on your bank's processing time.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

  • If our technician has already been dispatched to your location and you refuse the service.
  • If you provided an incorrect address or phone number, resulting in the technician being unable to reach you.
  • Once a package visit has been consumed (e.g., 1 out of 3 visits used), the entire plan cannot be refunded.

4. Refund for Failed Transactions

If your payment fails but money gets deducted from your account, it will be automatically reversed by Razorpay and your bank. If the amount is not refunded within 7 working days, please contact us with your transaction ID.

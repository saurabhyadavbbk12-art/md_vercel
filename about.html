Who We Are

Welcome to MD Enterprise , your most trusted destination for professional hardware tools, home fixtures, industrial equipment, and premium paints. Located in the heart of Barabanki, Uttar Pradesh, we have been serving homeowners, contractors, and businesses with top-quality products and reliable services for over 5+ years.

We are more than just a hardware shop. We are a complete solution provider. From selling high-grade plumbing materials to offering expert on-site repair and maintenance services, we ensure that your home or commercial space runs smoothly without any hassle.

We believe in transparency, quality, and customer satisfaction above everything else. Here is what makes us stand out:

✅ Genuine Products: We source our tools and hardware directly from authorized brands, ensuring you only get the best.

✅ Expert Services: Our team of experienced plumbers and technicians is always ready for doorstep visits, regular checkups, and emergency repairs.

✅ Free Price Comparison: Show us your plumber's slip, and we will provide a free brand-wise price comparison so you save money.

✅ Annual Scheduled Repair Packages: We offer flexible scheduled repair packages (like the Family Guard Plan) to give you priority support and scheduled maintenance visits.
